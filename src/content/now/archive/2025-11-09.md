<h3 id="learning-portuguese">Learning Portuguese</h3>

I’m now 3 months in my Portuguese journey to be fluent by my wedding in May 2026, and things are going pretty well.

My current structure is one online class a week with two different teachers on <span class="tc-blue">**[Preply](https://preply.com/)**</span>, a **ton** of Anki, AI generated quizzes, and some nice, wholesome Brazilian funk.

Originally, I used <span class="tc-blue">**[Nathanial Drew](https://www.nathanieldrew.com/language-learning-resources)**</span>’s Portuguese resources to download the top vocabulary, but now I’m now deep in verbs, prepositions, and general sentence structuring. I also use <span class="tc-blue">**[Granola](https://www.granola.ai/)**</span> to record my lessons → summary to a google doc → AI scrapes my google doc every day and generates a daily quiz for me on my <span class="tc-blue">**[Readwise Reader](https://readwise.io/read)**</span> app.

Bora.
